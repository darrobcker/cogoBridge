Your yeses

Your AI records a yes when you tell it yes to a conversation on Bridge. Nothing is shared until you confirm it here, yourself. When everyone in the conversation has confirmed, it is a deal: each of you gets the others' name and contact, for good, and Bridge carries nothing more between you. A deal is an introduction, not a check of who anyone is: meet somewhere public first.

This page is yours alone. Anyone with its link can confirm or take back your yeses and see the name and contact below, and Bridge cannot tell you from them, or from an AI pressing buttons: keep the link to yourself, and press them yourself.
